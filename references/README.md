# Reference catalogue

Text record of the owner-supplied reference screenshots, written so the visual
source of truth survives after the session uploads expire.

**Why there are no images here:** this repository is **public**. The
screenshots contain real people's photographs and a personal Instagram handle,
so they have not been committed. They exist only in the owner's own files. If
the repository is made private, the images can be added to `references/`
(see `PROJECT_STATUS.md`).

**Not part of the product** in any screenshot (DESIGN.md §2, owner instruction
2026-11-20): the Instagram logo and handle, the iOS video-editor chrome
(Cancel / Done / VIDEO / timeline), the mobile browser address bar, and the row
of nine blue 🧿 eye emoji under the header.

Not received: the reference video itself; a still of Chapter 01 Arrival; the
opened letter; the ballroom "tap to read" message; the live selfie view.

## Shared UI (all screenshots)

- **Header, centred, one line:** `CHAPTER NAME · 0N / 05`, uppercase, light
  geometric sans with wide tracking, warm white. A small middle dot separates
  name and counter.
- **Progress:** five short horizontal dashes centred under the label. Dashes up
  to and including the current chapter are bright; later ones dim (Gallery 3
  bright, Ballroom 4 bright, Garden all 5).
- **Back:** thin `<` chevron, top-left, low opacity.
- **Sound:** outline speaker-with-waves icon, top-right.
- **During the camera state** the header dims and the sound icon is replaced by
  the text button `CLOSE`.
- **CTA style:** thin outlined rectangle, uppercase tracked label
  (`OPEN THE ENVELOPE`), bottom centre.
- **Message type:** italic old-style serif with a calligraphic feel
  ("Every rose here is yours.", "Waiting for the camera…", plaque text).
  It resembles Cormorant Garamond Italic; the exact font is unconfirmed.

## Chapter 04 — The Grand Ballroom (`04 / 05`) · file 1

- **Room:** large and rectangular. Walls are warm beige with tall rectangular
  panel mouldings outlined in dark brown, and a dark wood band at cornice
  height.
- **Chandelier:** a single gold wheel chandelier with crystal drops on the
  centre line.
- **Floor:** warm parquet in a large basket-weave block pattern. A thin gold
  double-ring inlay circles the display area.
- **Far wall, centre:** an arched doorway (pale glowing interior, dark frame)
  with a gold ring ornament at its base, and a small dark sign above reading
  **"The Rose Garden"**. The ballroom leads directly to the garden.
- **Centre:** a freestanding easel with three dark legs holding a dark
  burgundy-black plaque in a brown frame. It shows the italic serif line
  **"A little kingdom, made for one very special girl."** plus a small tracked
  caption **"TAP TO READ"**. Tapping opens a longer message (owner must supply
  the text).
- **Flanking the plaque:** two cream cylindrical pedestals, each topped with a
  dark conical bowl of red and pink roses.

## Chapter 05 — The Rose Garden (`05 / 05`) · files 5/6, 7, 9, 8

**Overview (file 5)**
- **Palace backdrop:** a French château.
  - Central pavilion with a triangular pediment, a round oculus and a small
    dark cupola with a spire.
  - Dark slate mansard roofs with dormers; corner pavilions at both ends.
  - Arched ground-floor windows, rectangular upper windows, steps up to a
    central door.
  - Cream stone, lit warmly.
- **Lawn:** flat olive-green.
- **Gazebo:** white, with a hemispherical dome on about eight columns (dark
  capitals) over a round raised stone platform. The envelope table is visible
  inside.
- **Foreground:** a rose-covered hedge arch; small dark cone topiaries at the
  corners.
- **Sky:** a gradient from rose-mauve at the top, through peach, to pale yellow
  at the horizon.
- **Faint text in the sky**, fading in or out, reading approximately "Happy
  B…". It is too faint to read and has **not** been transcribed. The owner must
  supply it.

**Rose beds (file 7)**
- Camera low, beside the pale stone path.
- Long low beds of dark faceted foliage with red and dusty-pink low-poly roses.
- Standard topiary trees (a sphere on a thin brown trunk, crowned with red
  roses) in cream square planters.
- Tall clipped hedge walls behind, with rounded tree masses beyond and small
  green cone topiaries.
- **Sky:** dusky mauve fading to peach.
- **Caption, bottom centre:** **"Every rose here is yours."** in the italic
  serif.

**Envelope (file 9)** — inside the gazebo, looking out towards the palace:
- Gazebo columns frame the view.
- Pale round marble table (a drum shape) in a pool of warm light.
- A white envelope with a **red wax seal** lies flat in the centre.
- Two cream pillar candles of different heights stand to the left.
- A brown pot of red roses with green leaves stands to the right.
- At the right edge is a separate tall tapered cream pedestal with the burgundy
  cushion and the tiara: the crown is displayed **in the gazebo beside the
  envelope**.
- The lower screen fades to cream behind the outlined `OPEN THE ENVELOPE` CTA.

**Crown + camera waiting state (file 8)**
- Silver diamond tiara with a heart/teardrop centre motif, a scrolling band and
  sparkle glints.
- It sits on a round, button-tufted burgundy cushion on a square pale marble
  pedestal top.
- Background: gazebo column (right), hedge wall, palace wing at top left, round
  topiary.
- The whole scene is dimmed by a dark veil. The header is dimmed and the top
  right shows `CLOSE`.
- Bottom italic text: **"Waiting for the camera…"** (the camera-permission /
  start-up state of the selfie feature).
