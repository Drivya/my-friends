## Brand & Style
The design system channels the sovereign grandeur, classical equilibrium, and exquisite curation of Europe’s iconic royal residences and state museum galleries. Drawing direct inspiration from Versailles’ Hall of Mirrors, Buckingham Palace’s State Rooms, and the neoclassical galleries of the Louvre and Hermitage, the aesthetic conveys majesty, enduring permanence, and scholarly curation.

The visual style blends **Classical Architectural Elegance** with **Refined Tactile Minimalism**:
- Monumental verticality inspired by fluted pilasters, cornices, and gilded boiserie.
- Generous breathing space and symmetric balance reminiscent of neoclassical floor plans.
- Rich, velvety atmospheric depth counterweighted by delicate metallic hairline detailing and warm ambient candlelight radiance.
- Scholarly restraint: decorative motifs (such as fillets, fluted accents, and gilded filigree) act strictly as architectural framing rather than visual clutter.

## Colors
The palette evokes the nocturnal intimacy of candlelit state apartments and centuries-old walnut parquetry.

- **Primary (`#C6A15B` - Antique Gold):** Represents gilded leaf, patinated bronze ornamentation, and regal authority. Used for prominent active actions, key accents, focus rings, and high-level architectural rules.
- **Secondary (`#45151F` - Deep Burgundy):** Rooted in imperial velvet drapery, sovereign coronation mantles, and silk wall coverings. Serves as focal surfaces, interactive backdrops, badges, and high-impact structural panels.
- **Tertiary (`#E8D5A5` - Champagne Gold):** Luminous highlights, soft candlelight reflections, fine filigree strokes, and subtle hover accents.
- **Neutral (`#35221C` - Dark Walnut Wood):** Grounding architectural surface base that delivers warmth, gravitas, and deep contrast without resorting to sterile synthetic blacks.
- **Background & Canvas Tiers:**
  - Base canvas: `#231511` (Deep Charred Walnut) to `#35221C` (Dark Walnut Surface).
  - Raised surfaces & galleries: `#3F2B24` (Polished Walnut Panel).
  - High-tier overlays: `#45151F` (Crimson Velvet Chamber).
- **Text & Foreground:** 
  - Primary text: `#F4EBDD` (Warm Ivory) for pristine readability against dark timber.
  - Secondary text: `#E9DDC8` (Royal Cream) for subheadings, captions, and curatorial plaques.
  - Tertiary / Muted: `#A89885` (Weathered Stone) for metadata, footnotes, and disabled states.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy reflects the duality of royal proclamations and refined museum cataloguing:

- **Playfair Display (Headlines, Titles, Labels):** A high-contrast transitional serif delivering commanding poise. Used for stately headings, exhibit designations, and micro-labels. Small labels employ uppercase styling with open letter-spacing (`0.15em` to `0.2em`) evoking epigraphic Roman stone carving and gold leaf brass plaques.
- **Newsreader (Body & Curatorial Discourse):** An editorial serif engineered for prolonged immersion, curatorial narratives, provenance records, and historical treatises. Its organic cadence evokes hand-pressed archive paper.
- **Curatorial Microcopy:** Metadata such as dates, accession numbers, and inventory codes render in `label-sm` with uppercase treatment.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a rigorous classical architectural structure, balancing axial symmetry with generous negative space reminiscent of grand gallery vistas.

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout flanked by generous margins (`4.5rem`) and wide gutters (`2.5rem`), echoing the cadence of neoclassical peristyles. Maximum content container width is constrained to `1440px` to maintain dignified line lengths.
  - **Tablet (768px–1279px):** 8-column layout with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
  - **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Single-column stacks prioritize curatorial reading flow and museum artifact imagery.
- **Rhythm & Proportions:** Vertical rhythm adheres to multiples of `0.25rem` (4px base), expanding across major gallery sections with `space-xl` and monumental spacers (`4rem`–`6rem`) to create dramatic pauses between royal apartments and exhibitions.
- **Framing & Pilasters:** Major multi-column divisions are optionally delineated by 1px hairline vertical guides tinted in Antique Gold at low opacity (12%), visually representing structural fluted columns.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics the dimensional relief of classical boiserie, gilded picture frames, and the gentle diffusion of candlelight over oiled timber.

- **Candlelight Ambient Shadows:** Rather than cool, synthetic drop shadows, depth is manifested through warm, golden-amber ambient glows and deep umber falloffs:
  - **Layer 1 (Recessed/Flat):** Inset hairline stroke (`1px solid rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.12)`) without shadow.
  - **Layer 2 (Plinths & Gallery Cards):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(18, 10, 8, 0.65), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.2)`.
  - **Layer 3 (Modals, State Vitrines, Drawers):** `0 16px 48px -4px rgba(12, 6, 5, 0.85), 0 0 40px -10px rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.15), inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(232, 213, 165, 0.3)`.
- **Gilded Fillet Borders:** Surface boundaries rely on 1px dual-tone borders—an exterior stroke of patinated bronze (`rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.25)`) coupled with an internal ambient specular rim light (`rgba(232, 213, 165, 0.08)`).
- **Velvet Depth (Overlays):** Modal backdrops and drawer underlays utilize Deep Burgundy (`#45151F`) tinted at 85% opacity with an intense backdrop blur (`16px`), casting an atmospheric theater-like veil over background collections.

## Shapes
Geometry is disciplined, architectural, and restrained:

- **Level 1 (Soft):** Structural elements rely on an exacting, nearly-sharp `0.25rem` radius (`rounded-sm`), mirroring dressed ashlar stone, marble architraves, and mitered timber moulding. 
- **Large Panels & Vitrines:** Employ `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) exclusively to prevent visual harshness against high-contrast backgrounds while preserving neoclassical sobriety.
- **Architectural Motifs:** Decorative corners may incorporate subtle 45-degree chamfers or re-entrant notches on featured royal proclamations, certificate borders, and artifact vitrines.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Plinths
- **Primary Sovereign Button:**
  - Background: Linear gradient from Antique Gold (`#C6A15B`) to Champagne Gold (`#D6B573`).
  - Text: Dark Walnut Wood (`#35221C`), `label-md`, uppercase, bold letter spacing (`0.15em`).
  - Border: 1px solid `#E8D5A5`.
  - Hover: Subtle candle-glow illumination (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.45)`), background shifts slightly warmer.
- **Secondary Burgundy Velvet Button:**
  - Background: Deep Burgundy (`#45151F`).
  - Text: Warm Ivory (`#F4EBDD`), `label-md`.
  - Border: 1px solid `rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.35)`.
  - Hover: Border shifts to full Antique Gold (`#C6A15B`), subtle amber rim glow.
- **Ghost / Curatorial Text Button:**
  - Background: Transparent.
  - Text: Antique Gold (`#C6A15B`) with an animated 1px underline that expands outward from center on hover.

### Artifact Cards & Exhibition Vitrines
- **Surface:** Polished Dark Walnut (`#3F2B24`) framed with a 1px Antique Gold hairline border (`rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.2)`).
- **Image Treatment:** Artwork and historical photography sit behind a slight warm candlelight vignette, transitioning to full vivid contrast upon focus.
- **Plaque Footer:** Displays provenance data using `label-sm` in Royal Cream (`#E9DDC8`), separated from the body by a fine gilded moulding rule.

### Chips & Provenance Badges
- **Style:** Petite rectangular silhouettes with `0.25rem` radius, reminiscent of archival museum index tags.
- **Coloration:** Semi-translucent Deep Burgundy (`rgba(69, 21, 31, 0.6)`) or Dark Walnut (`rgba(53, 34, 28, 0.8)`).
- **Typography:** `label-sm`, uppercase with `0.18em` letter spacing, Antique Gold text.

### Curatorial Inputs & Search Fields
- **Container:** Recessed Dark Walnut base (`#231511`) with a 1px bottom border in Antique Gold (`rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.4)`).
- **Placeholder:** Weathered Stone (`#A89885`), Newsreader italic styling.
- **Active State:** Hairline border illuminates in vivid Antique Gold (`#C6A15B`) accompanied by a subtle upward warm candlelight plume shadow.

### Checkboxes & Selectors
- **Checkbox:** Square box with 1px soft corner. Unchecked: `1px solid rgba(198, 161, 91, 0.4)`. Checked: Antique Gold fill with Dark Walnut heraldic checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Concentric circular signets; selected state reveals an inner gilded patera dot illuminated with Champagne Gold.

### Lists & Catalogs
- Item entries are separated by gilded filigree divider lines (1px gradient fading to transparent at margins).
- Hovering an entry reveals an ambient Deep Burgundy highlight wash with an active gold indicator pip along the left margin.

### Specialized Component: Architectural Framing (Boiserie Dividers)
- Centered section dividers featuring a classical laurel rosette or gold diamond crest ornament flanked by dual horizontal hairlines in antique gold gradient.
