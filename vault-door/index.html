Voiceover script

    Production notes

    Timing
    Each chapter runs as long as its line takes to read at about 155 words a minute. Load a recorded voiceover and every chapter stretches in proportion to the recording's length.
    Narrate
    Uses your browser's built-in voice as a scratch track. Each chapter waits for its line to finish before moving on.
    The cut
    The upper-right quarter is removed down to the bolt centerline. Orange hatching marks cut steel, as on a machinist's section drawing.
    Simplified
    Real doors mount the lock cases on the inside face and block a boltwork bar rather than a central hub. Here all three blockers sit in one cavity so a single cut shows them together.
    Build
    Three.js r160, one HTML file, no image or audio assets. Every frame is a function of the timeline, so scrubbing backward rebuilds the scene exactly.
